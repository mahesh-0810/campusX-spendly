Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the app, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so that it can be shown back to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the core features of Spendly: logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and letting you sign in to your account. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the application. These providers only process data as needed to support the service and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
